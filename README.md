#  QuickBite Express Crisis Analysis using SQL


#  Project Overview

This project analyzes the business impact of the **June 2025 QuickBite Express crisis** using SQL. The objective is to identify how customer behavior, order volume, revenue, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction changed before and during the crisis.

The analysis simulates a real-world business scenario where a food delivery platform experienced a sharp decline in performance due to food safety concerns and operational disruptions. SQL was used to generate actionable insights that can support business recovery and decision-making.

---

#  Problem Statement

QuickBite Express is a Bengaluru-based food-tech startup founded in 2020 that connects customers with nearby restaurants and cloud kitchens.

In **June 2025**, the company faced a major crisis due to:

* A viral food safety incident involving partner restaurants
* A week-long delivery outage caused by monsoon disruptions
* Negative social media publicity
* Aggressive marketing campaigns from competitors

As a result:

* Customer engagement declined significantly.
* Daily order volume dropped sharply.
* Customer ratings and satisfaction decreased.
* Restaurant partners shifted to competing platforms.
* Customer acquisition costs increased.

The management team requested a detailed data analysis to understand the business impact and identify recovery opportunities.

#  Business Questions Solved

### 1. Monthly Order Trend

* Compared total orders before and during the crisis.
* Measured percentage decline in order volume.

---

### 2. City-wise Order Decline

* Identified the top 5 cities with the highest decline in customer orders.

---

### 3. Restaurant Performance

* Found the top 10 high-volume restaurants with the largest decline in orders during the crisis.

---

### 4. Cancellation Analysis

* Compared cancellation rates between pre-crisis and crisis periods.
* Identified cities with the highest cancellation rates.

---

### 5. Delivery SLA Analysis

* Measured average delivery time across business phases.
* Evaluated whether delivery performance deteriorated during the crisis.

---

### 6. Customer Ratings Analysis

* Tracked average customer ratings month-by-month.
* Identified months with the sharpest rating decline.

---

### 7. Customer Sentiment Analysis

* Extracted negative customer reviews during the crisis.
* Generated a Word Cloud in Power BI to identify the most frequent complaint keywords.

---

### 8. Revenue Impact

* Compared revenue before and during the crisis.
* Estimated total revenue loss using subtotal amount, discounts, and delivery fees.

---

### 9. Customer Loyalty Analysis

* Identified loyal customers (5+ pre-crisis orders) who stopped ordering during the crisis.
* Measured how many of those customers previously maintained an average rating above 4.5.

---

### 10. Customer Lifetime Value Analysis

* Identified the top 5% highest-spending customers before the crisis.
* Compared changes in order frequency and customer ratings during the crisis.
* Analyzed common patterns such as customer location, cuisine preference, and delivery performance.
